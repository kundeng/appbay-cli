Usage: appbay edge [options] [command]

Manage the edge: the proxy, and the users who sign in to your DEPLOYED APPS

Options:
  -h, --help         display help for command

Commands:
  users              Manage users who sign in to your DEPLOYED APPS (not to
                     AppBay itself)
  migrate [options]  Switch the ingress provider, validating first and restoring
                     the old edge on any failure
  help [command]     display help for command
